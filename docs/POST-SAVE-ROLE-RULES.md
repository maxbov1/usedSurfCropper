# Post-save listing-photo role rules

The human's saved shuffleboard arrangement is authoritative. The crop stage
must not regroup photos or override human group membership.

For each saved board group, exclude the inventory-card photo from the listing
photo count:

- Fewer than five listing photos: mark the group incomplete and require review.
- Exactly five listing photos: select the strongest side-profile photo; treat
  the other four as full-board photos.
- Six or more listing photos: select one strongest side-profile photo and one
  different strongest fin-detail photo; treat every remaining listing photo as
  full-board.

This is a constrained role assignment for cropping, not a second open-ended
classification or grouping pass. Uncertain photos remain in their human-saved
group and receive a crop-review warning rather than being moved or discarded.
